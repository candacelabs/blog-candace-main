## A public engineering notebook

Candace Labs documents experiments in systems software, self-hosting, privacy-minded tools, and practical AI infrastructure.

The useful parts belong here: design constraints, implementation notes, tradeoffs, and lessons that can be reused. Personal profiles and private infrastructure do not.

> Static by default. No trackers. No personal contact details. No hidden client-side machinery.
